How it works

1. Put it on the TVStart a game on the TV computer. The big screen shows the questions, the timer and a QR code.
2. Teams joinOne phone per team scans the code and picks a team name. Answers are typed on the phone.
3. Pens downTeams mark each other’s answers. Disagree? Dispute it, and the quizmaster rules on the big screen.
